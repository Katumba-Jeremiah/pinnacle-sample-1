About Pinnacle

Empowering farmers to run agriculture like a business.

Pinnacle Agribusiness Co. Ltd is an agribusiness consultancy firm dedicated to empowering farmers and agricultural businesses to achieve enhanced productivity and profitability, with a team of agronomists, vets, consultants and agricultural engineers serving farmers of every size.

Our journey

How we got here.

8 JULY 2019

Golden Green Thumbs Africa is conceived

Founded as a digital information platform promoting farming as a business — connecting farmers to commodity markets and up-to-date information on market trends, opportunities and agro-techniques.

REBRAND

Growth into Pinnacle Agribusiness Co. Ltd

New services and products were introduced, including backyard gardening, seedling production, greenhouse construction, piggery and a full agro-based consultancy practice. The firm operates as a partnership business.

TODAY

Incorporation in progress

Pinnacle is in the process of being legally incorporated and registered under the laws of local government and the Government of Uganda (GOU), while serving clients across the East African Community.

Vision

We exist to provide unmatched professional agribusiness solutions for enhanced productivity and maximisation of profits.

Mission

To transform African agriculture by empowering farmers through market-driven solutions, innovative technologies and comprehensive capacity building — delivering measurable improvements in productivity and profitability while promoting sustainable practices.
